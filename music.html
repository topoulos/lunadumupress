The Playlist

Every book in the Moon Gun Sam series has a listening companion — a curated list of songs that inspired the writing with notes on why each track was chosen and how it inspired the writing.

Music and album art belong to their respective artists and rights holders.

Links are provided for discovery and listening support through official platforms.

Support these wonderful artists and enjoy their works.

How It Works

Each entry includes the scene header from the book, the track and artist, official listening links, and expandable notes for readers who’ve finished the scene.
